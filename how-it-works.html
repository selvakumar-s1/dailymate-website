Simple 3-Step Process

Getting help shouldn't be complicated.

We’ve stripped away long sign-up loops, complex matching apps, and technical friction. Here is exactly how DailyMate works for you.

1

Submit Your Care Request

Navigate over to our Get Help portal. Chat directly with our conversational assistant to pick your service category and type a quick summary describing what you need sorted, along with your preferred timing.

2

We Match You With A Local Mate

Our underlying operational workspace handles the routing heavy lifting. We parse your task, locate available, background-checked assistants located right in your neighborhood community, and match your requirements with the perfect mate.

3

Task Done & Seamless Confirmation

We drop you a quick direct confirmation text message or a phone call on your phone to lock everything in. Your matched helper arrives on schedule, carries out your chores with expert care, and confirms completion smoothly. Easy!
